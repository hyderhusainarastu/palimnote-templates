# AGENTS.md

This repo is a catalog (an index) of copy-and-fill templates, plus two checklists in `checklists/`. Most templates live in their own repository.

## Rules
- Keep placeholders intact. In checklists they are written `{name}`; do not replace them in the committed files.
- Add a template to the README table only once its repository exists and is public.
- Do not claim a template was tested on an assistant unless that template's own README says so.
- Keep `description.txt` at 350 characters or fewer and `topics.txt` at 10 to 20 topics.
- Do not commit secrets, `.env` files or local absolute paths.
- Do not add AI-authorship wording to commits or docs.
