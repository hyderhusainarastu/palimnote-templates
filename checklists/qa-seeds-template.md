# Q&A seeds template

Use this to prepare answers to questions you expect after a talk or a defense. A seed is a short bullet you can answer from. It is a reminder of what your own paper already says, not a place to invent new claims.

Paste it into any assistant together with your paper, or fill it by hand.

## Rules

1. **Answer only from the paper.** Every seed points to a place in the paper (section, page or slide). If the paper has no answer, write **"The paper does not say"** and stop. Do not fill the gap with a plausible claim.
2. **Mark outside facts.** If a seed relies on a fact that is not in the paper (a scholar's exact position, a date, a statistic, a dispute about authorship), tag it **[outside the paper]**. Verify it yourself before you answer from it.
3. **Add no claims.** Seeds may rearrange and shorten what the paper argues. They may not strengthen it. If the paper promises something and does not develop it, say so and answer in terms of what the paper actually shows.
4. **Do not take sides on disputes the paper does not decide.** Name the dispute, say it does not bear on the argument, and move on.
5. **Keep a hostile reading.** For each likely objection, write the strongest version of it, then the answer from the paper.

## Template

Copy one block per expected question.

```
## [Question or topic, in the asker's likely words]

- Likely objection (strongest version):
- Answer from the paper (with location, e.g. section 3, p. 5):
- If the paper does not answer: "The paper does not say." Then: [what you can honestly offer, such as the scope of the claim]
- Outside facts used, each tagged [outside the paper] and checked on [date]:
- Do not claim:
```

## Prompt for an assistant

> Here is my paper. List the five to ten most likely objections from a hostile but fair audience. For each, write a Q&A seed using the template above. Quote the location in the paper for every answer. If the paper does not answer the objection, write "The paper does not say." Tag every fact that is not in the paper as [outside the paper]. Add no claims of your own.
